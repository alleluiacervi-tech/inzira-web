# What the official channels ask for, compared with our service list

Date of research: 2026-10-13. Sources are the institutions' own help pages where they could be read, and are named per row. Anything not found on an official page is marked **not verified** and left unchanged in the catalogue. Requirements change: staff must still check each one with the institution and mark the fee as checked in Admin › Services & prices.

## Findings that matter for how we work

1. **Irembo services require the applicant's own Irembo account** ("Applicants without an account cannot apply", or visit an Irembo agent). Inzira must decide, with legal advice, how it files for a customer. We must never ask for or store a customer's Irembo password.
2. **Irembo mostly asks for the ID number, not a photo.** It checks the number against the national ID system, which we cannot. Our ID photo is how Inzira's staff check who the customer is, so we keep asking for it, and the admin can see it (see below).
3. **Irembo uploads are limited** on land services: PDF files of 500 KB each. Staff must convert and compress customer photos before filing. Our own upload limit (10 MB, images or PDF) is fine for the customer side.
4. **RDB, RRA and RURA company services are not on Irembo's help centre.** RDB runs its own registration portal; RRA uses its own e-Tax and MyRRA. Land services and transport authorisation are on Irembo.

## What the admin sees: ID number and documents

- Every request already lists the documents its service asks for, with each file's review status. Staff with "Download customer documents" open the files (every open is written to the audit log).
- The full national ID number is hidden behind an audited "Show" button, on the request page and on the client page. Every use is written to the audit trail.
- Done: a separate permission, "View full ID and TIN" (`viewIdentity`), now controls that button. Owner, Administrator, Supervisor and Agent have it by default (agents only for their own cases, as before); Finance and Quality reviewers do not. The Owner can change this in Admin › Roles & people. Existing databases get these defaults from migration `0005`.
